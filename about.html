Who we are

We are an independent mobile studio driven by a simple belief: software should be crafted, not assembled. Every app we ship is something we would use ourselves, designed with care, built natively, and improved continuously.

We started AdvanTec because we wanted to build mobile apps the right way: native performance, thoughtful design, real AI integration, and privacy that does not require you to read the fine print.

More apps are always in the pipeline. We build each one with the same standards we set for ourselves on day one.
