Cookie Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

The short version

This site does not set analytics cookies, advertising cookies or any third-party cookies. The only data stored in your browser is your cookie consent choice, which is saved in localStorage and never sent to our server.

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file stored on your device by your browser when you visit a website. Cookies help sites remember your preferences and understand how visitors use the site. localStorage works in a similar way but the data is not sent to the server with every request.

2. What this site stores

Cookie consent choice

When you click OK or Decline on the cookie banner, we save your choice in your browser's localStorage under the key advantec_consent. This is so we do not show the banner again on your next visit. The stored value contains:

  • A version number
  • Whether you accepted analytics (currently always false because we have none)
  • Whether you accepted affiliate cookies (currently always false because we have none)
  • The date of your choice

This data stays in your browser. It is never sent to our server.

No other cookies

This site does not set any other cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, advertising networks, social media trackers or any other third-party tracking. The fonts are hosted on our own server, so no requests are made to Google Fonts.

3. GitHub Pages

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may set its own cookies for operational purposes such as load balancing. These are outside our control. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

4. How to manage cookies

You can clear your browser's localStorage and cookies at any time through your browser settings. You can also click the Cookie Settings link in the footer to reset your consent choice on this site.

5. If we add cookies in the future

If we add analytics or other non-essential cookies in the future, we will update this policy, add them to the consent banner and ask for your consent before setting them. Nothing will be pre-selected. You will always be able to decline.
