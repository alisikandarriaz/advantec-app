Data Deletion

How to Delete Your Data

AdvanTec · Last updated: September 2026

Key Points

  • You can delete your account directly from the app's Settings screen
  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone
  • All your data will be permanently removed within 30 days
  • If you can't access the app, you can request deletion by email below

1. How to Delete Your Account from the App

The fastest way to delete your account and all associated data is directly through the app:

  1. Open the app and go to Settings
  2. Scroll down and tap Account
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm when prompted

Your account and all data will be queued for permanent deletion. This process is irreversible.

2. What Happens to Your Data

Once you delete your account, the following happens:

  • Your profile information is removed immediately
  • All personal data, preferences, and app content are permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Any active subscriptions will be cancelled — though you should also cancel via Google Play to avoid further charges
  • We retain no backup of your data after the 30-day processing period

3. Can't Access the App?

If you are unable to access the app or your account, you can request data deletion using the form below. We will process your request within 30 days as required by GDPR.
