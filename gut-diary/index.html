A symptom diary for Android. Log bowel movements, symptoms, food, medication, sleep, stress and weight. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What It Records

Your daily diary, on your phone

Gut Diary records what you enter and shows it back to you. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

  • Bowel movements with Bristol type, urgency, pain, blood and colour
  • Symptoms and their severity
  • Food entries and tags
  • Medication doses and schedules
  • Sleep, stress and weight (optional)
  • Your own custom fields
  • Photos attached to bowel entries
  • Notes for your appointment

When an entry matches a fixed rule, such as blood, black or tarry stool, or a weight drop when weight tracking is on, the app shows a notice suggesting you contact a doctor. That notice comes from simple fixed rules, not an assessment.

Plans

Free and paid

Everything you need to keep a daily diary is free. The paid plan adds more tools for longer-term tracking.

Free

  • Unlimited logging
  • Fixed notices for flagged entries
  • Today screen and History (last 30 days)
  • Good days count
  • Home screen widgets
  • Local encrypted backup
  • CSV export
  • Delete all data

Paid

  • Clinician PDF report
  • Insights screen
  • History beyond 30 days
  • Photo attachments
  • More than two custom fields

Available as monthly, annual or lifetime. Prices shown by Google Play. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. If a subscription ends, nothing is locked and no data is removed.

Privacy

Your entries stay on your phone

Gut Diary has no account, no cloud sync, no AI, no analytics, no advertising and no internet permission. Your entries never leave your device unless you send them yourself.
