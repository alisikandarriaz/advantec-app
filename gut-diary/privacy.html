Gut Diary

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Who publishes Gut Diary

Gut Diary is published by AdvanTec. For anything in this policy, email info@advantec.app.

The short version

Your diary stays on your phone. Gut Diary has no account, no internet permission, no analytics, no advertising and no AI. We never receive your entries and cannot see them.

What Gut Diary stores, and where

Gut Diary is an Android symptom diary for adults aged 18 and over. Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Bowel movements, including Bristol type, urgency, pain, blood and colour
  • Symptoms and their severity, food entries and tags, medication doses, sleep, stress, weight if you turn weight tracking on, and your own custom fields
  • Photos you attach to bowel entries, copied into the app's private storage
  • Notes for your appointment, and your settings
  • A record of which entries matched a fixed notice rule

This is health data, a special category of personal data under data protection law. Because it never leaves your device through the app, we do not collect or process it.

What Gut Diary does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The app does not have the Android permission to do so.
  • It has no account, sign-in or user identifier.
  • It contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
  • It does not share or sell data. There is nothing for it to share.

When data leaves your device

Data leaves your phone only when you choose to send it:

  • The clinician report. You open it through Android's print dialog, and print it or save it as a PDF wherever you choose.
  • CSV export. You choose where the file is saved.
  • Encrypted backup. The backup is an encrypted file you save wherever you choose, protected by a password you pick. The password cannot be recovered. Photos are not included in backups.
  • Share. The share action sends only the Play Store link, not any diary data.
  • Contact. The contact action opens your email app with only the app version and Android version pre-filled. We receive only what you choose to send.

Once a file is saved or shared outside the app, it is under your control and the privacy terms of wherever you put it. Deleting data in Gut Diary does not delete files you saved elsewhere.

If you uninstall the app or lose your phone without a saved backup, your entries are gone and nobody can recover them.

Backups and moving to a new phone

Android's automatic backup and phone-to-phone transfer do not include this app's data. To move to a new phone, use Back up to a file in Settings on the old phone and Restore from a backup on the new one.

Widgets

The home screen widgets show today's counts on your home screen, where anyone who can see the screen can read them. You can remove the widgets at any time.

If you email us

If you email info@advantec.app, we hold your email address and message so we can reply. You can ask us to delete them at any time by emailing the same address.

Links in the app

Links in the app open in your browser or in the Play Store. The app itself makes no internet connection.

Purchases

The paid plan is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under Google's own privacy policy. We do not receive your card details. Google gives us order records such as an order number, the product, the date, the price and the country. We use these only to provide the plan, handle refunds and keep records the law requires.

Payments are handled by Google Play. I never receive your card details, and Google's own privacy policy covers payment information.

Google Play's billing component keeps a small diagnostic log on your phone. The app has no internet permission, so that log cannot be sent anywhere, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Deleting your data

Open Settings in the app and choose Delete all data. This removes every entry, photo, setting and consent choice from your device immediately and permanently. Uninstalling the app also removes your data.

Because we hold no diary data, there is nothing for us to send or delete on request. Backups you saved outside the app are yours to delete.

To ask about purchase records, email info@advantec.app.

Your rights

You can ask us about any personal data we hold about you and ask for access, correction, deletion or restriction, or object to its use. You can complain to your data protection authority. For the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). In the EU, contact your local authority.

Children

Gut Diary is for adults aged 18 and over.
