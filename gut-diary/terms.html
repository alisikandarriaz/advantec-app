2. What Gut Diary is

Gut Diary is a personal diary. It records what you enter and shows it back to you, including as a report you can print or save. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. It does not suggest causes or recommend changes to diet or medication.

When an entry matches a fixed rule, the app shows a notice suggesting you contact a doctor. The app shows this notice when you log blood, black or tarry stool, or a weight drop when weight tracking is on. The notice comes from simple fixed rules and is not an assessment. The rules are not exhaustive. Not seeing a notice never means that a symptom is not serious.

Always talk to a qualified clinician about your health. If you think you need urgent care, contact your local emergency services directly. Do not wait for the app.
