Percentyl

A baby growth tracker for Android. Log weight, height and head circumference. See exactly where your child sits against WHO, CDC and INTERGROWTH-21st growth standards, with every number traceable to its official source.

What It Tracks

Your child's growth, on your phone

Percentyl records what you enter and plots it against published growth standards. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose anything.

  • Weight, length or height, and head circumference
  • Percentile position against WHO, CDC and INTERGROWTH-21st standards
  • Corrected age for preterm babies
  • Developmental milestones
  • Photos you attach to measurements
  • Every reference is traceable to its official published source

All growth data stays on your device. Nothing about your child's records is ever sent anywhere.

Privacy

Your child's data stays on your phone

Percentyl has no account, no cloud sync and no advertising. All growth data is stored only on your device. The only network activity is Google Play checking a subscription status.
