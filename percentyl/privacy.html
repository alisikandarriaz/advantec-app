Percentyl

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

Who publishes Percentyl

Percentyl is published by AdvanTec. For anything in this policy, email info@advantec.app.

The short version

Your child's growth data stays on your phone. Percentyl has no account, no cloud sync, no advertising and no AI. We never receive your child's measurements and cannot see them.

Who this app is for

Percentyl is built for parents and carers to track their own child's growth. The parent or carer is the person operating the app. The app is not designed for, marketed to, or intended to be used by children.

What Percentyl stores, and where

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your child's name, date of birth, sex and gestational age
  • Weight, length or height, and head circumference measurements
  • Developmental milestone records
  • Photos you attach to measurements, copied into the app's private storage
  • Your settings and preferences

This includes data about a child, which may be considered a special category of personal data under data protection law. Because it never leaves your device through the app, we do not collect or process it.

Network use

Percentyl requests internet access as an Android permission. This is used only by the Google Play Billing library, which contacts Google's servers to check subscription status.

Percentyl's own code does not send or receive any data over the network. Your child's growth data never leaves your device through the app.

What Percentyl does not do

  • It does not send your child's growth data anywhere. The app's own code makes no network requests.
  • It has no account, sign-in or user identifier.
  • It has no cloud backup or cloud sync.
  • It contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
  • It does not share or sell data.

When data leaves your device

Data leaves your phone only when you choose to send it:

  • Export. You choose where the file is saved.
  • Contact. The contact action opens your email app with only the app version and Android version pre-filled. We receive only what you choose to send.

Once a file is saved or shared outside the app, it is under your control and the privacy terms of wherever you put it. Deleting data in Percentyl does not delete files you saved elsewhere.

If you uninstall the app or lose your phone, your data is gone and nobody can recover it.

If you email us

If you email info@advantec.app, we hold your email address and message so we can reply. You can ask us to delete them at any time by emailing the same address.

Links in the app

Links in the app open in your browser or in the Play Store. The app's own code makes no internet connection beyond the Google Play Billing check described above.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. We never receive your card details, and Google's own privacy policy covers payment information.

Google Play's billing component keeps a small diagnostic log on your phone. The only network activity this creates is Google Play checking subscription status. That log is deleted when you uninstall the app.

In this release, no purchases are available yet. All Pro features are shown as coming soon.

Deleting your data

Open Settings in the app and choose Delete all data. This removes every measurement, photo, child profile, setting and preference from your device immediately and permanently. Uninstalling the app also removes your data.

Because we hold no growth data, there is nothing for us to send or delete on request. There is no account to delete since there is no account.

Your rights

You can ask us about any personal data we hold about you and ask for access, correction, deletion or restriction, or object to its use. You can complain to your data protection authority. For the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). In the EU, contact your local authority.

Children's data

Percentyl records data about children, entered by their parent or carer. This data is stored only on the parent's or carer's device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party through the app.

Note: This policy is pending a legal review against Google Play's Families Policy and applicable child data regulations before the app goes live.
