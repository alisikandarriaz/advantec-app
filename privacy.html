1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how we handle personal data on the AdvanTec website (advantec.app). Each of our apps has its own privacy policy linked from its own page.

2. What data we collect and why

Contact form

When you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address and message. We use this to reply to you. The lawful basis is legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR): answering enquiries we receive.

The form is handled by Formspree, which receives and stores the submission so we can read and reply to it. Formspree's servers are in the United States. We rely on Formspree's Data Processing Agreement and Standard Contractual Clauses for this transfer.

Server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address and request headers when you visit any page. This is standard web hosting. The lawful basis is legitimate interest (keeping the site running and secure). GitHub's servers are in the United States. We rely on GitHub's Data Processing Agreement for this transfer.

Domain provider

The domain advantec.app is registered with Namecheap. Namecheap handles DNS resolution but does not receive personal data from visitors beyond what DNS resolution requires (your IP address).

3. Cookies

This site does not set analytics cookies or advertising cookies. The only data stored in your browser is your cookie consent choice, saved in localStorage. It is not a cookie and is not sent to our server. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not run analytics on this website.
  • We do not use advertising or affiliate links.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone for marketing.
  • We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

5. How long we keep your data

Contact form submissions are kept until we have answered your enquiry and for up to 12 months afterwards, then deleted. GitHub server logs are retained under GitHub's own retention policy.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format

To exercise any right, email info@advantec.app. We will respond within one month.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

7. Third-party links

This site links to Google Play and other external sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for their content.

8. Children

This website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through this site.
