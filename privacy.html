Privacy Policy

AdvanTec · Last updated: January 1, 2025 · United Kingdom

1. Who We Are

AdvanTec is an independent mobile app studio based in the United Kingdom. This Privacy Policy applies to all apps we publish, including FocusAI, TimeDot, and Digestive Health, as well as this website. We are committed to GDPR compliance.

Contact: info@advantec.app

2. Data We Collect

Account Data

  • Email address, display name, profile photo (optional)
  • Authentication method (email or Google Sign-In)

App Usage Data

  • Tasks, sessions, settings, and preferences you create within the app
  • Anonymous analytics: device model, OS version, feature usage. This cannot be linked back to you personally.

Advertising Data (Free Tier Only)

  • Android Advertising ID, used by Google AdMob to serve ads
  • Opt out via Android Settings, Privacy, Ads
  • Upgrading to a paid tier removes all ads and advertising data collection

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Delivering app features and cloud sync
  • AI responses via third-party AI APIs
  • Push notifications for reminders
  • Anonymous analytics to improve our apps
  • Billing verification via Google Play

Legal bases under UK GDPR: contract performance, legitimate interests (analytics), consent (optional integrations), legal obligation.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase for authentication, cloud storage, and push notifications
  • Google AI APIs for AI-powered features within apps
  • Google AdMob for ads on free tiers only
  • Google Play Billing for subscription management

We never sell your personal data.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Local data is stored encrypted on your device. Cloud data for paid users is stored in Google Cloud infrastructure in the EU region. Data is retained until you delete your account, after which all data is removed within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Delete your account and all associated data
  • Portability: Export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any right, email info@advantec.app. We respond within 30 days.

You may also contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

7. Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us at info@advantec.app for immediate deletion.

8. Security

All data in transit is encrypted via TLS 1.2 or higher. Local databases use Android built-in encryption. Access controls restrict cloud data to the authenticated account owner only.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of significant changes via in-app notification. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.

10. Contact

For all privacy questions and data requests: info@advantec.app
