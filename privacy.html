Privacy Policy

AdvanTec · Last updated: January 2025 · United Kingdom

At AdvanTec, your privacy is something we take seriously. This page explains clearly what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and how we keep it safe. By using our website or any of our apps, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Your Consent

By accessing or using our website and apps, you agree to the terms set out in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not continue using our services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect two types of information depending on how you use our services.

Personal Information

  • Your name and email address when you create an account or contact us
  • Information you provide when filling out forms on our website or apps
  • Billing details processed securely through Google Play for paid subscriptions

Non-Personal Information

  • Device information such as model, operating system version, and screen size
  • Anonymous usage data including which features you use and how often
  • Log data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited on our website
  • Cookies and similar technologies used to improve your browsing experience

Neither personal nor non-personal information is ever sold or shared with unauthorised third parties.

3. Cookies

We use cookies on our website to improve your experience and understand how visitors interact with our content. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us analyse traffic and remember your preferences. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time, though doing so may affect how parts of the website function. By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of cookies.

4. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Deliver and continuously improve our apps and services
  • Personalise your in-app experience based on your preferences
  • Process transactions and send related notifications
  • Respond to messages, questions, and support requests
  • Analyse usage patterns to make better product decisions
  • Meet legal obligations where required

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to anyone. We may share limited data with trusted third-party providers who help us operate our services, strictly for that purpose. These include:

  • Google Firebase for authentication, cloud storage, and push notifications
  • Google AI services for AI-powered features within our apps
  • Google AdMob for serving ads on free tiers only
  • Google Play Billing for managing subscriptions

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of AdvanTec and its users.

6. Data Storage and Security

We apply strong security measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. Data stored locally on your device is encrypted using Android's built-in encryption. Cloud data for paid users is stored in Google Cloud infrastructure within the EU region. That said, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely without risk, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data at any time. To exercise any of the following rights, contact us at info@advantec.app and we will respond within 30 days.

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Rectification: Ask us to correct any inaccurate information
  • Erasure: Request that we delete your account and all associated data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests

If you are in the UK, you may also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

8. Third-Party Links

Our website or apps may occasionally contain links to external websites. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those sites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party sites before sharing your information with them.

9. Children

Our apps are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through our services, please contact us immediately and we will remove it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of significant changes through an in-app notification. The date at the top of this page always reflects the most recent version. We recommend checking back periodically to stay informed.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please get in touch through our Contact page or email us directly at info@advantec.app. We are happy to help.
